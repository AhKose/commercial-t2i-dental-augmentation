# Results

This folder contains the numerical results reported in the manuscript.

## Files

- `primary_augmentation_utility.csv`: baseline, GPT-35, Nano-35, and Mixed-70 results.
- `matched_budget_results.csv`: N=10 and N=30 matched-budget source-comparison results.
- `expert_validation_results.csv`: expert-filtered versus count-matched random subset results.

Primary augmentation-utility results are reported as mean ± standard deviation across three patient-level cross-validation folds. Matched-budget and expert-validation results are aggregated across folds and random-seed repetitions.
